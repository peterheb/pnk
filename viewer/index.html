settings

Documents name fonts your device may not have. With this on, the viewer asks fonts.googleapis.com for a close substitute, so the page looks closer to the original: Google sees your IP address and the font family names, never the document. With it off nothing leaves your browser, and missing fonts fall back to whatever your browser picks.

        privacy & terms
        
pnk.vu runs entirely in your browser: documents you open are parsed locally and never uploaded.
        We collect no personal data. Our host, Cloudflare, keeps standard server-side logs and anonymous
        analytics. When a document names a font you do not have, the viewer asks Google Fonts for a close
        substitute, so Google sees your IP address and the font names — never the document; the
        settings menu turns that off. Provided as-is, without warranty of any kind.
